Hi, I'm Mathan.

[//]: # (Where I'm from and where I studied)
I'm from [Madurai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurai), Tamil Nadu, India. I grew up and did all my schooling there, then moved to Coimbatore for five years to do an integrated M.Sc. in Data Science at [PSG College of Technology](https://www.psgtech.edu/).

[//]: # (Work, at a glance)
These days I'm a founding engineer at [DataGenie](https://www.datagenie.ai/). It's a small team full of people with very different perspectives, and there's always something new to figure out. Before that, in my third year of college, I interned at [ZeroDown](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zerodown), a YC startup. That was my first taste of working with experienced engineers, in a real production environment, at a startup.

[//]: # (How this all started)
Outside of work, I just like building and tinkering. That's how I started, actually. My first real project was a [social network](https://github.com/mathanamathav/socialnetwork), basically a mini Facebook clone, that a few of us built while learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript from CS50. I still remember the late nights getting the chat to work, then the rush of deploying it and nudging everyone to sign up. That same circle kept building after that: hackathon projects, side projects, and [Bunker](https://github.com/mathanamathav/bunker-api), an attendance tracker for those of us who liked to skip class. Some juniors still find it useful.

[//]: # (How I feel about AI and where the industry is going)
It's a strange, exciting time to be a software developer. It's easy to get overwhelmed, but AI and the new coding tools make building far more accessible than before. To me they're less a crutch and more a personal assistant, like Jarvis to Iron Man. Coding agents like Claude Code are reliable enough to build interesting things with, and with the right planning, getting a proof of concept started is easy. The rest is time and your own curiosity.

[//]: # (Why this site exists)
I'm building this site to document what I work on, so that in a few years I can look back and see how I, and my thinking, have changed.
