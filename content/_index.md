Hi, I'm Mathan.

I grew up in Madurai and went to school there, then spent five years in Coimbatore doing an integrated M.Sc. Data Science at [PSG College of Technology](https://www.psgtech.edu/).

So far I've had two stints in industry. The first was an internship in my third year, working as a software developer at a startup called [ZeroDown](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zerodown). These days I'm a founding engineer at [DataGenie](https://www.datagenie.ai/) — a small, collaborative team where I'm constantly learning. It's unconventional, full of interesting people with very different perspectives, and there's always something new to figure out.

Outside of work, I just like building and tinkering — that's how I started, actually. My first real project was a [social network](https://github.com/mathanamathav/socialnetwork), basically a mini Facebook clone. I picked up HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from the famous cs50 course just to figure out how to put it together. I still remember the late nights getting the chat to work, then the rush of deploying it and nudging my friends to sign up and see it live.

It's a strange, exciting time to be a software developer. It's easy to get overwhelmed, but I think AI and the new coding tools make building far more accessible than before. To me they're less a crutch and more a personal assistant like Jarvis to Iron Man. Coding agents like Claude are genuinely reliable for building interesting things; the rest is just time and your own curiosity. They're enablers.

I'm building this site to document what I work on, so I can look back in a few years and see how my thinking and I have changed over time.
