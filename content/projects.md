### IntersectX

An LLM chat and investment research platform. Founders pitch their companies, and VCs research them with help from agents that each cover one area: finance, market, team and risk. Prize winner at Agno's Global Agent Hackathon, 2025.\
[Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WZ5jvBFEOSM) · [Code](https://github.com/mathanamathav/VentureInsights-Backend) · [Winners](https://www.agno.com/articles/global-agent-hackathon-winners)

### DoodleDraw

A drawing game in the browser. A CNN we trained guesses your doodle in real time, and you score a point when it matches the prompt.\
[Code](https://github.com/Ashishkumaraswamy/doodledraw)

### CodeForHer

A prototype chat and voice assistant for guided learning and safer commutes. Won first prize at Locus's Code For Her hackathon, 2025.\
[Code](https://github.com/Ashishkumaraswamy/CodeForHer-Backend) · [Article](https://www.hindustantimes.com/genesis/safer-roads-by-code-india-s-next-gen-hack-transforming-safety-101750678135616.html)
